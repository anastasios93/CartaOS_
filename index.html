The Problem

Brilliant science. Broken dealmaking.

Biotechs with world-class science sit across the table from pharma companies that have entire departments dedicated to deal intelligence, pricing models, and negotiation strategy. The information asymmetry is massive — and it costs biotechs billions in undervalued deals, collapsed negotiations, and medicines that never reach patients.

What breaks today

✕

Deals collapse late in negotiations because biotechs lack credible market data and comparable deal terms

✕

Boards can't align on go/no-go decisions — diligence takes months, not days

✕

Biotechs accept unfavorable milestone structures because they can't benchmark against the full deal landscape

✕

Medicines are delayed or never launched in key markets due to pricing and market-access uncertainty

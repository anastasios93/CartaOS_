The Problem

No operating system for the most important transactions in medicine.

These organizations collaborate through licensing agreements, co-development partnerships, and acquisitions — the mechanism through which scientific discovery becomes medicine. Yet the system governing these partnerships is still extremely primitive. The information asymmetry between large pharma and emerging biotech is massive — and it costs the industry billions in undervalued deals, collapsed negotiations, and medicines that never reach patients.

How deals are managed today

✕

Spreadsheets and fragmented databases with no unified view of the deal landscape

✕

Email chains and consultant reports that go stale before they're finished

✕

Personal networks as the primary source of partnership intelligence

✕

Days of manual research for benchmarks that should take minutes

Introducing Deal Twin

Transaction intelligence across the deal lifecycle — powered by Deal Twin.

Input the characteristics of your therapeutic asset and CartaOS generates a Deal Twin — a predictive model of how similar assets have historically been partnered. CartaOS analyzes licensing agreements, M&A transactions, clinical trial trajectories, and regulatory outcomes to help you find the right partners and structure deals with data-driven insight.

How it works — An example

NovaBio is a 30-person biotech with a single Head of BD — no deal team, no in-house analysts. They have a Phase II bispecific antibody in non-small cell lung cancer and need a pharma partner to fund Phase III and commercialize globally.

The Head of BD inputs their asset into CartaOS. Within minutes, Deal Twin maps the entire deal landscape — comparable transactions, predicted economics, the right partners, and a full negotiation-ready package. Here's what happens:

01
⚖
Data-driven deal benchmarking
Instead of weeks pulling comps from scattered databases, CartaOS instantly surfaces 14 comparable licensing deals for bispecific antibodies in solid tumors — what the upfronts looked like, how milestones were staged, what royalty tiers were standard, and which deal patterns drove the most value.
02
☌
Find the right partners faster
The board wants a partner shortlist by Friday. CartaOS identifies which pharma companies have historically licensed bispecific antibodies in NSCLC — ranked by deal activity, geographic fit, and strategic alignment — so NovaBio focuses outreach where it actually matters.
03
⚡
Your analytical companion in every deal
A term sheet arrives with a $50M upfront and tiered royalties. Is that competitive? CartaOS gives instant benchmarks: how the structure compares to recent transactions, where NovaBio has leverage to push, and what the other side is likely thinking — 24/7, not just when an advisor is available.

Everything lives in one workspace — seamlessly integrated with Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. The board, legal counsel, and scientific advisors each get access to the right information at the right time, without digging through email threads.
